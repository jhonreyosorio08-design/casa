Our story

Born from a love of feeding people well.

“The best evenings are never rushed.”

Casa Sonata began with a simple dream: create the kind of restaurant that feels like the home of a dear friend with exceptional taste. In 2018, founders Elena and Marco opened our doors on a quiet Milanese street.

Chef Marco’s cooking is rooted in Italian tradition but guided by the moment—what the farmers bring, what the sea offers, and what makes the room feel alive. We make pasta by hand, pour with an open hand, and believe every detail matters.
